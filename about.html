About this Project

Why this platform was created.

The Problem

In most towns in Bangladesh, day labourers face difficulties finding work every day. Many workers have to stand at local labour points every morning and wait for employers to hire them. They often do not know what work is available, what the expected daily wage is, or whether they will receive the payment that was promised.

On the other hand, employers also face difficulties finding suitable workers when they need them. They have to visit labour points and search manually for workers with the required skills and experience.

Another major problem is the involvement of middlemen, who often take a portion of workers' wages, reducing their already limited income.

These problems create difficulties for both workers and employers. There is a need for a simple and accessible platform where employers can directly post job opportunities and workers can find and apply for suitable work.
